## 1. Mission and Objectives

### Mission Statement
Our mission is to collaboratively explore and apply organizational learning principles through scholarly research and practical application, fostering a culture of continuous improvement, shared knowledge, and professional growth while supporting each team member's doctoral journey in applied learning science.

### Team Objectives (SMART)

**Objective 1: Academic Excellence**
- **Specific:** Complete all three group projects with a minimum average score of 90% or higher
- **Measurable:** Track individual assignment grades and team project rubric scores
- **Achievable:** Allocate 8-10 hours per week per team member for group work
- **Relevant:** Directly supports course outcomes and doctoral program requirements
- **Time-bound:** Achieve by December 21, 2025 (end of 7-week course)

**Objective 2: Collaborative Learning**
- **Specific:** Each team member will lead one project component based on their expertise and share key learnings with the team
- **Measurable:** Complete peer evaluations after each of the 3 group projects
- **Achievable:** Rotate leadership across projects; document lessons learned
- **Relevant:** Aligns with organizational learning principles and course focus on collaborative practice
- **Time-bound:** Complete rotation by Week 7 (December 2025)

**Objective 3: Professional Development**
- **Specific:** Apply improvement science frameworks to our team processes, conducting one reflection session after each project
- **Measurable:** Document 3 team process improvements across the semester
- **Achievable:** Schedule 30-minute retrospective meetings after each project submission
- **Relevant:** Demonstrates application of course concepts to our own team as a learning organization
- **Time-bound:** Complete all 3 reflections by December 21, 2025

**Objective 4: Individual E-Portfolio Development**
- **Specific:** Each member maintains an updated E-Portfolio with all collaborative work, personal reflections, and evidence of learning
- **Measurable:** Upload team documents within 24 hours of final submission
- **Achievable:** Use consistent folder structure and naming conventions
- **Relevant:** Required for Final Capstone Project and professional development
- **Time-bound:** Ongoing throughout the course; final review by December 15, 2025

## 3. Ground Rules and Quality Standards

### Ground Rules

**Professionalism:**
1. Arrive to scheduled meetings on time (7:00 PM CT / 8:00 PM ET Tuesdays)
2. Complete assigned tasks by agreed-upon deadlines
3. Communicate proactively about challenges or delays (minimum 24 hours notice)
4. Provide constructive feedback using "I" statements and solution-oriented language
5. Honor confidentiality of team discussions and personal disclosures

**Respect & Inclusion:**
1. Listen actively without interrupting; ensure all voices are heard in meetings
2. Value diverse perspectives and lived experiences
3. Assume positive intent; address concerns directly and privately before escalating
4. Use inclusive language; respect pronouns and cultural differences
5. Acknowledge contributions of all team members in final submissions

**Collaboration & Equity:**
1. Distribute workload equitably (approximately 25% responsibility per member)
2. Rotate leadership and documentation roles across the 3 projects
3. Share relevant resources and knowledge openly with the team
4. Ask for help when needed; offer help proactively
5. Celebrate milestones and successes together

**Academic Integrity:**
1. Properly cite all sources using APA 7th edition format
2. Distinguish between collaborative work and individual contributions
3. Do not share team materials outside the group without unanimous consent
4. Report any concerns about academic integrity to the team immediately
5. Adhere to University of Miami Honor Code and TAL 602 course policies

### Quality Standards

**Submission Standards:**
1. **72-Hour Review Cycle:** All draft submissions completed 72 hours (3 days) before deadline
2. **Minimum 2 Peer Reviews:** Each draft reviewed by at least 2 team members before submission
3. **APA 7 Compliance:** All formatting, citations, and references checked using APA 7th edition
4. **Rubric Alignment:** Self-assess against rubric before peer review; document how each criterion is met
5. **Professional Presentation:** Error-free grammar, consistent formatting, clear headings, professional tone

**Research & Evidence Standards:**
1. Use minimum 3 peer-reviewed sources per project (unless specified otherwise)
2. Prioritize sources from last 5 years (2020-2025) when possible
3. Connect research explicitly to TAL 602 course concepts and readings
4. Integrate multiple perspectives; avoid over-reliance on single authors/frameworks

**Process Standards:**
1. **Project Kickoff:** Within 24 hours of project assignment, create shared outline and task breakdown
2. **Progress Check-ins:** Share status updates every Tuesday and Friday via Teams
3. **Iterative Drafts:** Create minimum 2 drafts before final submission
4. **Retrospective:** Complete 30-minute team reflection within 48 hours of each submission

**Version Control:**
1. Use Google Drive version history; never delete previous versions
2. Document major changes in file name (e.g., `_Draft1`, `_Draft2`, `_Final`)
3. Create backup copies in individual E-Portfolios before submission
4. Tag final version as `_SUBMITTED_YYYYMMDD` after Blackboard upload

### Conflict Resolution Process

Our team commits to addressing conflicts proactively, professionally, and in alignment with organizational learning principles of reflective practice and dialogue.

#### Step 1: Direct Communication (First 24-48 Hours)

**When to use:** Misunderstandings, minor disagreements, unclear expectations

**Process:**
1. **Cool-down period:** If emotions are high, wait 2-4 hours before responding
2. **Private 1:1 conversation:** Address concern directly with involved party via Zoom or phone
3. **Use "I" statements:** Focus on impact rather than intent (e.g., "I felt confused when..." vs. "You didn't...")
4. **Seek understanding:** Ask clarifying questions; assume positive intent
5. **Agree on solution:** Document agreed-upon changes and communicate to team if needed
6. **Timeline:** Resolve within 48 hours of issue arising

**Example conflicts:** Missed deadline, different interpretation of assignment, communication breakdown

#### Step 2: Facilitated Team Discussion (48-72 Hours)

**When to use:** Conflicts affecting multiple team members, unresolved Step 1 issues, disagreements about approach

**Process:**
1. **Request mediation:** Any team member can request facilitated discussion in Teams #general
2. **Neutral facilitator:** Identify team member not directly involved to facilitate (or rotate role)
3. **Structured discussion:** Schedule 30-minute Zoom meeting within 48 hours
4. **Agenda format:**
   - Each party shares perspective (2-3 minutes, uninterrupted)
   - Identify shared goals and common ground
   - Brainstorm 3-5 potential solutions
   - Vote on solution or create hybrid approach
5. **Document outcome:** Facilitator records agreements in Teams and follows up in 72 hours
6. **Timeline:** Resolve within 72 hours of escalation

**Example conflicts:** Disagreement about project direction, unequal workload distribution, repeated missed commitments

#### Step 3: Instructor Involvement (After 72 Hours)

**When to use:** Unresolved conflicts after Steps 1-2, serious academic integrity concerns, pattern of behavior affecting team function

**Process:**
1. **Team consensus:** All team members agree to involve instructor (or 3 of 4 members in extreme cases)
2. **Written summary:** Prepare brief, objective description of:
   - Nature of conflict
   - Steps already taken (evidence of attempts to resolve)
   - Specific request for instructor support
3. **Email instructor:** Use subject line "TAL 602 Team Conflict Mediation Request"
4. **Schedule meeting:** Attend scheduled meeting with instructor (all relevant parties present)
5. **Implement solution:** Follow instructor's guidance and document outcome
6. **Follow-up:** Check in with team 1 week after resolution to assess effectiveness

**Example conflicts:** Persistent non-participation, academic integrity violations, interpersonal conflicts affecting mental health

### Conflict Prevention Strategies

1. **Clarity from the start:** Use SMART language for all task assignments
2. **Regular check-ins:** Weekly status updates prevent surprises
3. **Psychological safety:** Encourage speaking up early when concerns arise
4. **Retrospectives:** Learn from each project to improve team processes
5. **Workload monitoring:** QA Lead checks for equitable distribution before each submission
